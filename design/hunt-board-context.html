Wasteland — the hunt board

A turn-based hunt in a text-and-hairlines survival game. The player presses one of four things; each press resolves instantly. There is no timer anywhere in this block — it is the only thing on the site that does not count down, and it sits where the last press left it for a night or a week.

the rules, so the numbers mean something

Ground 0–2. Two presses of Close the distance to be within reach. You cannot strike from further.
Alarm 0–3. At 3 a deer or hare bolts and the hunt is over; a boar turns to face you instead.
The shot is not a chance. It lands if ground is 2 and alarm is no higher than the weapon allows — bare hands 0, scrap spear 1, hunting bow 2.
It is — the animal's attention, on a three-beat cycle that turns on every press: feeding → lifting → watching. Closing costs 0, 1, 2 respectively.
Wind — behind / across / ahead, fixed for the hunt, shifts every closing cost by −1 / 0 / +1.
Press 5 of them, and then the light goes and the animal is gone.
The whole decision: waiting for a cheap beat is how you close for nothing, and waiting is how you run out of presses.
